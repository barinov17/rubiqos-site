Physical AI

Robots, autonomous vehicles and vessels that run the island day: guest pods and shuttles, service carts and trucks, delivery robots, shuttle and supply boats. This section maps each fleet type of the master plan to the platforms that exist on the market today, and shows where each one fits in the resort operation.

v1 · market map, 2026-10-17. Fleet types and counts come from the Fleet Register of the master plan; candidates are platforms with a commercial launch or an open order book. Per-type detail (capacity, autonomy, price, references) comes next.

Fleet type → platforms on the market

Nine fleet types the operation needs, and who builds them. Every type is covered by shipping hardware; autonomy on land and water is commercial, but so far in cities and at a handful of resorts.
