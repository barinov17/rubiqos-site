Platform

How RubiqOS works.

Three layers on one platform, shared by the developer, every operator and the guest.

One platform

Staff get the task and the tools. Operators keep their own systems. The guest gets one app for the whole property, with the room key inside.

Orchestration engine

The platform plans each task, routes it to the right robot or person and measures the result against its budget line.

Shared fleet

Leased through hardware partners across the six domains. One fleet serves every operator on the property.
