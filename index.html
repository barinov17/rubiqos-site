Problem

High operating costs.

~63%
of a resort’s revenue goes to operating costs.

A resort needs a large imported staff plus the housing, transport, utilities and logistics to support it. Payroll is the largest line: 28% of revenue. Admin and maintenance add 16%. Together they are 44% of revenue and seven tenths of all operating costs, and they are the lines automation reaches first. Sales and supplies take 19%; the remaining 37% is operating profit.

Solution

Solutions for resorts.

One platform. Shared fleets. Every operator.
