The dispatcher of the live demo.

A proposal for discussion. The dispatcher is the operator's view of the same island the demo shows: every intent from the guest app, every job for a person or a machine, and the state of the fleet, the venues and the staff, on one screen. Below is the composition we suggest, with sample data.

Status: concept. The live demo already produces the data this screen needs (fleet jobs, battery, people by place, events, counters). The next step is to decide which of these blocks the first version shows and who operates it: a human dispatcher, the AI, or both.

Composition

1 · Island map

The master plan, live: vehicles, people, boats, venues with load, drop-offs and docks. Layers by group and zone, click = object card, time scrubber for the day.

2 · Request queue

Intents from the guest app and the staff: buggy call, room service, luggage, maintenance, medical. Each with SLA, assigned asset and ETA. The AI assigns, the operator can override.

3 · Fleet board

Every buggy, truck, robot, cart and boat: job, position, battery, next charge, open faults. Pre-positioning for the next event wave.

4 · People and staff

Guests by place (scaled from the plan), staff on shift by post, next shift change, open posts, tender arrivals due.

5 · Programme timeline

Today's events and logistics windows: what starts in the next hour, what it needs (buggies, staff, equipment), what it changes in routing.

6 · Alerts and exceptions

Weather and sea state, delayed deliveries, low batteries, blocked roads, medical calls, VIP arrivals. One click to the object on the map.

7 · Scenario controls

Time and speed, inject an event (storm, medical, buy-out), switch the island to low-season mode, compare with and without automation.

8 · KPIs

Pier-to-suite time, buggy wait, delivery time, work orders closed before guests return, staff on the move, energy and water today.

Open questions

Who operates it

One human dispatcher per shift with the AI proposing every assignment, or the AI dispatching alone with a human on exceptions only. The demo can show both modes.

What the first version shows

Our suggestion: map, request queue, fleet board and alerts. People, programme and KPIs in the second step.

Data model

Places, assets, people, intents, jobs, events. The demo already carries places (from the model), assets and events; intents and jobs are implicit in the scenario and need to become explicit objects.
