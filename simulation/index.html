How the simulation moves

The live demo is not an animation. Every person, vehicle, boat and the helicopter decides its next trip on its own, from the time of day, the event programme and the rules on this page. The system only dispatches: pods answer calls, trucks meet the supply vessel, the marina assigns berths. This page is the rulebook behind the map, in the same words the code uses.

v1.1 · 2026-10-19 (operators added). Rules read from the demo source (prototype/resort-glb) and the vault notes Resort Population and Guest Day, Fleet Plan and Vehicle Scenarios, Event Programme, Helipad Model. Numbers are the demo's, at reduced scale where stated.

Six principles

What holds for everything on the map.

1 · Time

One real second is one simulated minute. The day opens at 06:40; the week opens on Monday. Staff shifts change at 07:00, 15:00 and 23:00. Hours of the day decide what people want and what the fleet does.

2 · Three networks, strictly

Wheeled vehicles move only on roads and drop-off pads. People move only on walkways, promenades and village ground. Boats move only on a 10 m water grid with one cell of clearance off land, piers and the breakwater. Nothing cuts across a lawn or a quay.

3 · Routes, not lines

Every trip is an A* route on its own network. If no route exists, the trip does not happen: the actor waits and chooses again. A missing link is fixed in the master-plan model (a footpath, a road join), never faked in the code.

4 · Decisions are local

Each actor has one function: where next? It reads the clock, rolls a share from the guest-day table and looks at the events running now. There is no central script, except pod jobs raised by the system (helicopter transfers, pod calls).

5 · Energy

Pods, shuttles, carts, trucks and robots drain their battery while moving (a full charge is about 2.5 simulated hours of driving). Under 30 % they drive to their charger and charge 40 minutes to 100 %. About a fifth of the fleet is charging at any time.

6 · Scale

The plan is 1,000 guests on a peak day. The demo moves 330 people and a reduced fleet so the map stays readable; the dashboard scales what you see back to the plan.

Calling a pod

30 % of trips longer than 250 m go by guest pod: the person walks to the nearest drop-off, calls, and waits. Riders at the same stop for the same hop share one pod and are hidden aboard. After 40 minutes without a pod the person walks instead.

Arrivals and departures

Each mainland shuttle lands 12–50 people at the guest dock: half day visitors, 30 % hotel guests, 20 % members; after 17:00 all hotel guests. Hotel guests take a pod from the Marina stop to the hotel; the others walk to the village or the club. Departing day visitors and members walk to the dock and board. Helicopter guests appear in the Beach Hotel lobby after the pod transfer.

Events pull people

From 15 minutes before the start to the end, an event is a destination: 30 % of the people it is open to (everyone, residents only, or hotel guests only) choose its venue over the day table. Capacity is the venue's; events under 15 places do not pull the crowd.

On the water

A 10 m water grid, A* routes, one cell of clearance. Inside the marina the boats use the drawn fairway network: a trunk channel inside the breakwater, a spur up every lane between the pontoons, and the exit leg to the south-west sea point.

In the air

A1 · VIP helicopter charter

Five calls a day: 09:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00, 2–6 guests each. Approach from the WSW over the sea into the trade wind, 12 minutes on the pad, climb-out to the NW over the water. On landing the system raises two pod jobs: one brings the arriving guests from drop-off DO-23 to the Beach Hotel, another collects the departing guests at the hotel and brings them up before take-off.

What the system does

Dispatches pods to calls and jobs. Assigns berths from AIS tracks. Raises work orders from sensors. Books the excursion boat by the programme. Counts trips, deliveries, work orders, arrivals and departures for the dashboard under the map. The guest only taps; no phone calls.

Reading the map

Hover a person or a vehicle for its card and current task. Click a vehicle and the camera locks on it; orbit and zoom still work around it; click elsewhere, Overview, Live camera or Escape release it. Click a zone in the layers panel to filter; Live camera follows the scripted story.
