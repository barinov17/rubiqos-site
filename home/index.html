Questions we get.

Is it safe to run robots among guests?

Most work is back of house and at night. On guest paths robots move at walking speed on fixed routes under remote supervision; every route runs in the simulation first.

What happens when a robot fails?

The task returns to the queue, to a person or another robot; a shared fleet has spare capacity by design. Service and spares are the hardware partner’s duty under the lease.

How long does deployment take?

In a resort in design, with the master plan, so nothing is retrofitted. On an operating resort, one domain first, then more season by season.

Who pays, and when?

The developer, from payroll it no longer carries: a design-phase fee, a platform fee and outcome fees per job done. Robots are leased, so no fleet sits on the balance sheet.

Which robots are supported?

Any robot with an open interface. The register has 26 types, from guest pods and shuttle boats to mowers and patrol drones. We build the middleware, not the robots.

What data leaves the resort?

Operational data stays with the resort; the platform keeps task, route and telemetry logs to plan and to bill. Guest data follows the operator’s own policy.
