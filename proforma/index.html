Proforma: what the project consists of.

RubiqOS (working name DestinationOS) is the operating system of a resort or a mixed-use destination: a guest app, an agent layer that dispatches people and machines, and physical AI on the roads and the water. This site collects what the project consists of today: the master plan, the operating model, the product concepts, the market, the live demo and the pitch.

How the system is built

1 · Guest experience layer

A mobile app for guests, members and residents. Curation over catalogue; the Butler and the map are the only entry points. Every tap becomes an intent the system can fulfil.

2 · Agent layer

The Butler AI, the operator console for human concierges and the dispatcher that turns intents into jobs for people and machines. Humans stay in the loop, invisibly to the guest.

3 · Physical AI layer

Autonomous buggies, service trucks, delivery robots, service carts and boats, plus the state of the island: energy, water, weather, tides. The live demo shows one day of this layer on the master plan.
